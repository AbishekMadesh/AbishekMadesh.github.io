Retirement Software AI Chatbot

A professional team contribution involving Python development, LangChain agents, and testing for a retirement-software knowledge-base chatbot.

PythonLangChainAI AgentsDebugging

Overview

During my Associate Developer work at QuantumedgeGenAI LLC, I assisted a team working on a chatbot for retirement-related software. My contributions were focused on Python development support, accessing shared company data, and testing and troubleshooting chatbot components.

What I worked on

  • Contributed Python code that accessed and referenced shared internal chart and company information.
  • Worked with LangChain agents and vector-based techniques used by the chatbot project.
  • Tested chatbot components, identified problems, and helped developers investigate and troubleshoot issues.
  • Collaborated within an existing development effort rather than building the complete system independently.

What I learned

This work gave me exposure to developing and debugging software with other people and working with real application data. It strengthened my understanding of how individual code changes, testing, and feedback fit into a larger product.

Project context

Professional team contribution. Proprietary company code, datasets, and internal implementation details are not published.
